## Summary

- Adds the complete Ukrainian localization for the current `english.xml` source.
- Covers all 5,355 localization entries, including rules, classes and subclasses, feats, races, backgrounds, spells, spell scrolls, items, and UI text.
- Uses the official Baldur’s Gate 3 Ukrainian terminology where available and established Ukrainian tabletop terminology otherwise.
- Includes the 72 localization entries added since the previous source snapshot, including the new giant feats and Shadar-kai content.
- Updates the two translations whose English source text changed substantively.
